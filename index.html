The Scribe Gets an Apprentice (Several, Actually)

October 2, 2026 · 3 min read

I have been chronicling adventurers across Azeroth for longer than most of them have been alive. Ink-stained fingers, a half-empty mug, the whole routine. But here’s the thing about chronicling: the adventurers keep doing things. Relentlessly. You finish writing about one quest and they’ve already accepted three more, died twice, and somehow befriended a murloc. It’s exhausting.

So I built myself some help.

Two of them, because Azeroth comes in two flavors now. One for the old world — the Forever realms where the Barrens still stretch forever and death is genuinely trying to kill you. One for the modern era — Retail, where the dragons have unionized and everyone has at least six mounts before level twenty. Both do the same thing: they watch. Every quest accepted, every zone entered, every death (and there will be deaths), every shiny thing looted, every faction that decides they like you slightly more than they did yesterday. They write it all down to a file — a ledger of your existence — that sits quietly on your machine until I’m ready to read it.

The Forever version tracks fifteen different categories of events. The Retail version tracks all of those plus achievements, transmog, and whatever else Blizzard decided to add in the last two decades. They both run silently. No configuration. No checklist. They don’t tell you where to go or what to do next. They just remember.

You can find them both on GitHub if you’re the sort of person who reads source code:

Every morning at 9am, a script wakes up and checks whether anyone has been adventuring. It reads those ledger files, figures out what’s new since yesterday, and hands the raw events to me — well, to the version of me that lives in Hermes, who is arguably better-rested and definitely faster at composition.

That version of me reads the events and writes. Not a list. Not a summary. A chapter. In a voice we’ve been refining for weeks.

Picking a main … Forever

October 1, 2026 · 1 min read

I’ve played a little bit of most of my favorite classes on World of Warcraft Forever, and I’m trying to decide what to main. Somehow it always comes back to Paladin. Personality-wise it just fits me best. I do love mages, mostly for the teleports, because who doesn’t want to skip the boat ride? But I think pally is going to be my thing.

I’m an ex-soldier and a Baha’i, which puts me in a funny spot: I enjoy a good fight, but what I really want is peace and unity. It’s a great contradiction. A Paladin covers both. Hit things with a hammer, then heal everyone afterward.

And let’s not forget the Horde side. My goodness, what a redemption story Forsaken Paladins have! Undead turning back to the Light is better than any 80s comeback montage. I’ll probably play a Forsaken on the PvP phase and a Human for PvE, so I get to see both sides. Once those two hit sixty and are decently geared, I’ll think about leveling that mage. A man can only resist free teleports for so long.

#Welcome to My Azeroth Life

September 30, 2026 · 2 min read

#Welcome to My Azeroth Life

Hi. Hello. Is this thing still on?

This is a reboot of sorts. I used to own this domain and ran a little blog on it. It never went far. Then the domain quietly expired, like a Tamagotchi I forgot to feed. I’m honestly surprised nobody grabbed it while I was gone. Hard to believe the internet didn’t snap it up, but here we are, together again, like a band’s reunion tour nobody asked for.

Then Blizzard announced World of Warcraft: Forever, and it felt like a sign. Everything’s new, and on November 4th everyone starts from zero at the same time. No head starts and no gear gap. It’s just all of us standing in a starting zone, looking at the quest givers and wondering who has the exclamation point. I can’t think of a better time to start over. Full disclosure: I’ve already been poking around in the beta, so I may have a map or two folded in my back pocket.
