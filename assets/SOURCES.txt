Game artwork (art/loading-painting, mountain-lighthouse, forest-gate, orc-camp,
floating-isles): from Blizzard's public news article "World of Warcraft: Forever
Found Photos Panel Recap"
https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24304071/world-of-warcraft-forever-found-photos-panel-recap
re-encoded to WebP.

Faction emblems (art/alliance-crest, art/horde-sigil) and cursor (ui/gauntlet.png):
from warcraft.wiki.gg (File:AllianceLogo.png, File:HordeLogo.png,
File:Pointer gauntlet on 32x32.png), scaled.

Item icons (icons/*.jpg): from Blizzard's public icon CDN,
https://render.worldofwarcraft.com/us/icons/56/<name>.jpg, unmodified.
