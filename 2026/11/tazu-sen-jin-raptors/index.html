Eggs for Xar’Ti

By Tim Apple · November 15, 2026 · 3 min read

The makrura came up out of the surf on Darkspear Strand like the sea had sent its rubbish back, all claws and clacking plates, and Tazu met them in the shallows with lightning in one hand and a stone mace in the other. Somewhere in the middle of it, with the water pink in the evening light and a clacker going still at his feet, the spirits leaned on him again. Light rose through him from the sand up. He came out of it stronger than he went in, the way you do when the world decides you have earned a little more of it. The makrura did not notice. Makrura notice very little.

He was there for Master Vornal. Vornal is the old alchemist of Sen’jin Village, whose eyes are failing and whose temper is not. He has never taken an apprentice, because no troll or orc has ever come to him worthy enough. “Are you worthy?” he asks the young ones who wander up to his fire. “Yes, of course you are… of course you think you are.” Then he sends them down to the shore for intact makrura eyes and vials of crawler mucus, which is the kind of errand that tells a master a great deal about a student.

Tazu brought him four eyes and eight vials. “Well done, young one,” Vornal said. “Well done indeed.” Then he paid him in really sticky glue, ten pots of it, which the chronicler suspects is Vornal’s idea of a joke. Orgrimmar and the Darkspear both took note. Vornal has a saying for those who keep him waiting: time is the true test of your strength and will, and if you lose patience or give in to weakness, your true self will be shown. The earth at Spirit Rock told Tazu much the same thing, not long ago. Be patient. Remain strong. The spirits, it seems, have been talking to the old men of Sen’jin.

Then there was Xar’Ti. Every raptor in the pens outside Sen’jin is one Xar’Ti trained, and they come from all over Kalimdor, but some of the finest come from the Bloodtalons on the Echo Isles. One of those, the Bloodtalon Matriarch, had grown too savage to leave at the village’s borders. She had killed trolls from Sen’jin. Xar’Ti wanted her dead, and wanted her eggs brought home, so the hatchlings could be raised as allies instead of enemies.

The chronicler did not see the Matriarch fall. He only saw Tazu walk up to the pens at sunset with her eggs, and Xar’Ti take them, and the swift raptors in their saddles turn their heads to watch. There is something about it the chronicler keeps coming back to. A mother killed for being too dangerous, and her children carried home to be taught better. The Darkspear are a practical people. Xar’Ti gave Tazu a pair of raptorscale trousers for his trouble, and the chronicler hopes, for the hatchlings’ sake, that none of them ever learns where the leather came from.

Three hundred and eighty-five coppers by nightfall. No deaths. He made camp in Durotar.

A report is owed to Orgnil in Razor Hill. A peon’s burden is ready to be set down. And somewhere in Durotar the Kolkar centaurs have drawn up plans to strike at the Valley of Trials, Sen’jin Village and Orgrimmar itself. Tazu has burned one of them. Two are still out there, and the centaurs do not know yet that a patient troll is looking for them.
