Steel Over Sparkles: Shuffling My Azeroth Lineup

By Tim Apple · November 1, 2026 · 2 min read

WoW Forever is almost here, and I’ve been doing some soul-searching. It’s the kind you do between loading screens, staring at a character select page and asking yourself who you really are. Apparently I’m someone who likes to hit things.

For Forever, I’ve decided to go analog. That means Warrior, Rogue, Hunter and Paladin. No fireballs, no portals, and no standing in the back reading from a glowing book while someone else gets punched. The Paladin is the only one who dabbles in the Light, but he’s still a soldier first. He just brought a prayer to the sword fight. In the end, it fits my personality. Yes, I’m aware I also made the Cudsong brothers. Consistency is a virtue I’m still leveling.

Who are they, exactly? You’ll have to wait. Opening day is a name-grab free-for-all, and I’m not announcing anyone until the names are safely mine after November 4. I’ll tell you this much: I’m pretty sure they’ll all be Horde. Shocking, I know.

Retail isn’t going anywhere, but it’s changing roles. I’ll follow the same idea there, using only the classes Forever doesn’t have. That mostly leaves two: the Demon Hunter and the Death Knight. Conveniently, they’re also the two classes most likely to show up to a party looking like they lost an argument with the afterlife.

So the Retail chronicler will mostly follow two new faces:

  • Lemmindris, my Night Elf Demon Hunter (Alliance), who gave up a perfectly good soul for a set of wings and a blindfold.
  • Ghoulsbie, my Death Knight (Horde), who is technically dead and still more motivated than I am on a Monday.

Their introduction episode is coming as an extra Retail episode, this evening or tomorrow.

I’ll still play Retail, but expect a lot more Forever content than Retail for a while. Azeroth is big enough for both of me. My free time, less so.

See you in the queue on November 4.
