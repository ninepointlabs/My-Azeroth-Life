To Adventurers on Linux

OmaForge

A World of Warcraft addon manager for Linux.
Retail, Classic and Forever, under Wine or Proton.

Finds your game in Lutris, Steam, Bottles, Heroic or ~/.wine. Searches CurseForge, WoWInterface and GitHub together, and backs up your WTF folder before bulk updates. Wears your Omarchy theme; runs on any Linux desktop.

Free and open source · MIT licence

To Priests of WoW Forever

Andric’s Addon

The Priest companion for World of Warcraft: Forever.
Holy, Discipline or Shadow: pick your path.

What to cast, what to wear, where to quest and what to train, in one window that shows you what to do next. A Fortitude button buffs the next friendly player with each press: one press, one cast, and nothing casts by itself.

Free and open source · MIT · WoW Forever only

andricsaddon.myazerothlife.com

To Mages of WoW Forever

Parzelle’s Addon

The Mage companion for World of Warcraft: Forever.
Arcane, Fire or Frost: pick your path.

What to cast, what to wear, where to quest, what to train and where to teleport, in one window that shows you what to do next. Its buttons conjure water, food and a mana gem, buff Arcane Intellect and hand out: one press, one cast, and nothing casts by itself.
